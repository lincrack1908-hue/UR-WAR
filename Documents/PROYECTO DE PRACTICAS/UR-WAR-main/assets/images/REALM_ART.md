# Rediseño integral de UR WAR

Ilustraciones originales generadas con la herramienta integrada de imágenes de Codex. No se usaron personajes ni recursos de otras franquicias.

- `heroes_atlas.png`: cuatro retratos, recortados en tiempo de renderizado mediante AtlasArt.
- `territories_atlas.png`: bosque, nieve y territorio volcánico, recortados en tiempo de renderizado.
- `kingdom_menu.png`: fondo del inicio conservado.
- `../fonts/Cinzel.ttf`: fuente de Google Fonts, licencia SIL OFL incluida en OFL-Cinzel.txt.

Los actores de combate son ilustraciones vectoriales 2D de Canvas, con movimiento continuo de piernas, brazos, capas y armas. No son modelos 3D ni recortes animados de los retratos. Los identificadores históricos PixelSpriteData se conservan para compatibilidad con la lógica de entidades; la presentación ya no pinta sus matrices de píxeles.

## Prompt de héroes

Original dark fantasy game character selection art for UR WAR. Wide 4:1 contact sheet, four EXACT equal-width vertical panels arranged left to right, each with one full-body hero centered in their own quarter, entire head and boots visible and comfortable margins. Left quarter: imperial knight in engraved steel plate, closed helmet with red plume, dark oxblood cape, longsword and shield. Second quarter: female sylvan ranger with forest green hood, leather armor, longbow, quiver. Third quarter: arcane male mage in deep muted violet robes, hood, silver rune trims, glowing staff with small amber flame. Fourth quarter: female sacred guardian with ivory and antique gold armor and robe, luminous small gold staff, no wings. All are adults, dignified grounded heroic anatomy, 3/4 front view facing viewer. High quality painterly game key art, realistic cloth and forged metal, subtle rim lighting, dark charcoal fog background in each quarter blending seamlessly between panels. Consistent camera distance and scale, restrained aged gold palette, detailed faces but no oversharpening. No text, no labels, no frame, no UI, no existing franchise characters. NOT pixel art, not chibi, not cartoon.

## Prompt de territorios

Create original dark fantasy strategy game campaign environment concept art, panoramic triptych of THREE EXACT EQUAL WIDTH sections left to right with no borders. Left third: misty war-torn evergreen valley, medieval walled village, red war banners, distant ruined battlements, smoke and golden dusk. Middle third: snowy mountain pass, ancient stone fortress, blue-grey ice cliffs, military encampment torchlights and blowing snow. Right third: volcanic obsidian stronghold, glowing red lava fissures, ruined black towers, ash sky and subdued amber embers. All sections landscape compositions, environment only no people in foreground. Painterly premium game art, naturalistic detail, restrained charcoal and antique gold, atmospheric mist, readable architectural silhouettes. Cohesive with medieval knight/ranger/mage/cleric village defense game. No text, no UI, no labels, no pixel art, no modern objects. 3:1 wide image.
